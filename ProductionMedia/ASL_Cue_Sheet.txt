ASL Cue Sheet — I Can See Now (Accessible Edition)
Style: Clear, warm, expressive. Keep hands within chest-to-shoulder frame. Pace to match narration.
Interpreter window: bottom-left; high contrast clothing; soft smile.

0:00–0:05  “Even without sight…”
- Emphasize SEE (point from eyes outward), then TOUCH (tap hand), SOUND (gesture to ear), LOVE (heart sign).

0:05–0:15  “This is I Can See Now…”
- Introduce APP (book-to-screen movement), CHILDREN BLIND/LOW-VISION (proper signs), WELCOME (invite).

0:15–0:35  “Designed for hospitals…”
- HOSPITAL (cross sign), THERAPY (T-hand movement), FAMILY (circle), LEARN/SMILE (sign both).

0:35–0:55  “Every station comes to life…”
- WORLD MOVE (wave), RIVER FLOW (wave hands), BUTTERFLY (thumbs hooked flutter), BRAILLE (dot read), VOICES SING/CLAP/READ.

0:55–1:20  Station examples
- Left↔Right track with head/hand shift. ORANGE/ LAVENDER (sign scents). READ-WITH-HAND (trace line).

1:20–1:40  FaithFriend Mom
- MOTHER HELP (open hand heart), ASK-QUESTION (brow up), ANSWER-KIND (soft palms).

1:40–1:58  Baby Mode
- BABY HOLD (rock), HEART-BEAT (tap chest), TOGETHER (hands join).

1:58–2:15  Hospital & privacy
- PRIVATE (hand close), OFFLINE (cut internet), CAPTION (type text), SIGN-LANGUAGE (ASL sign).

2:15–2:30  Closing
- HEALING BEGIN CONNECTION (link hands), EVERY CHILD (sweep), FEEL LIGHT (open to sky, glow).
